### 🐺 [Huskii](https://huskii.be)

A digital studio from Diepenbeek. Web development, managed hosting, API integrations and drone video — small team, short lines, no fluff. Websites that load fast and do what they promise.

### 🎯 [Expliciet](https://expliciet.be)

*Understand to stand out.* A full-service marketing agency in Hasselt. I work on the web and tech side: WordPress builds and maintenance, hosting and servers, automation and the AI tooling that keeps a busy agency running smoothly.

### 🐾 [Ik ben vermist](https://ikbenvermist.be)

The free central reporting point for lost and found pets in Belgium. Report a missing pet in two minutes, get a ready-to-print poster instantly and let neighbours help search. Running for over ten years and still growing.
